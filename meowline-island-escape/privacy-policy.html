Privacy Policy — Meowline: Island Escape

Effective date: October 27, 2026

Meowline: Island Escape (the “App”) is a cat-themed island adventure and arrow-path puzzle game. For privacy questions or requests, contact leo0912v@gmail.com.

1. Information stored on your device

The App does not require an account, sign-in, phone number, or registration. Game progress and preferences are stored locally on your device. This can include campaign levels and ratings, daily activity, check-in progress, sticker and collection state, hint and guide inventories, sound, music, vibration, display settings, and your optional-services choice.

The App does not currently provide account-based cloud saves or account recovery. If you delete the App or its local data, or change devices, locally stored progress may not be recoverable.

The App also writes a local event log inside its private app data. It can contain gameplay event names, level or mode identifiers, timestamps, and advertising event details such as format, placement, outcome, reward type, and reported ad revenue. The log remains on the device; optional analytics providers receive events only when those services are enabled and you have agreed to optional services.

2. Advertising, attribution, analytics, and remote settings

A release may enable optional third-party services. The actual services and information processed depend on the release build, platform, region, and vendor configuration:

  • AppLovin MAX may provide rewarded, interstitial, banner, or app-open advertisements. AppLovin and its mediated ad partners may process device and app information, advertising identifiers, IP address, ad requests, impressions, clicks, reward events, ad revenue, and related technical information.
  • AppsFlyer may provide install attribution, campaign and ad measurement, session information, and ad-revenue measurement. Depending on configuration, it may process device or advertising identifiers, IP address, app information, and events sent by the App.
  • GameAnalytics may receive gameplay progression events (such as level starts, completions, and failures), advertising lifecycle and revenue events, and technical information needed to provide analytics. If enabled, its Remote Config service may return game and advertising settings. Retrieved settings are cached locally and are not cloud saves of your game progress.
  • Optional iOS service integrations identified in the App as CXPO and TSA may make network requests or load web content when enabled for a release. Their providers may process technical or interaction information under their own terms. TSA requires a provider app key to initialize; DBB is not bundled in the current project setup.

For provider information, see the AppLovin Privacy Policy, AppsFlyer Services Privacy Policy, and GameAnalytics Privacy Notice. Services are not necessarily active in every release.

3. Consent and tracking choices

Before optional advertising, attribution, analytics, and remote settings start, the App presents a choice. If you agree, services configured for your release may operate as described above. If you continue without optional services, the core puzzle game remains playable and those integrations are not started by the App.

You can change your choice in the App’s Settings. Withdrawing consent stops new optional requests and application-level event submissions and removes the App’s cached remote settings. Information already sent to a provider remains subject to that provider’s retention and deletion practices. Some optional iOS services may require you to close and reopen the App to finish stopping them.

On supported iOS versions, Apple may separately ask whether the App can track activity across other companies’ apps and websites. This system permission is controlled in iOS Settings. Declining it does not prevent you from playing and does not necessarily disable every service that can operate without cross-app tracking permission.

4. How information is used

Where applicable, information is used to save gameplay locally, operate puzzle and collection features, provide and measure advertisements, attribute installs and campaigns, understand level progression, deliver remote settings, improve the App, and respond to support or privacy messages.

5. Sharing

The App does not operate an account database or sell information through an account service. When optional services are enabled, relevant information may be shared with the service provider, its processors, and advertising partners to provide ads, attribution, analytics, or measurement. Information included in an email is handled by the email services used to send and receive it. Each provider’s privacy notice describes its own processing.

6. Retention and privacy requests

Local progress and event logs remain in the App’s private data on your device until they are removed through device controls, by clearing app data, or by deleting the App. Clearing local data may also remove your progress. The App removes its remote-settings cache when you withdraw optional-services consent.

Third-party providers handle data under their own retention and request procedures. For a privacy question or request, email leo0912v@gmail.com with “Privacy Request” in the subject. Please do not email passwords, payment-card details, government ID numbers, or other sensitive information.

7. Children

The App is a casual puzzle game and does not ask players to create accounts or submit personal details. The applicable age rating and child-directed settings depend on the release and the requirements of its distribution platform and region. Parents or guardians with a privacy concern may contact us using the address below.

8. Security and international processing

We use reasonable safeguards for information under our control. No app, device, or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure. Third-party providers may process information in countries other than your own under their own policies and safeguards.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the App or its data practices change. We will update the effective date and publish the current version at the public Privacy Policy URL associated with the App.
