Meowline: Island Escape — Technical Support

Need help with Meowline: Island Escape? Email leo0912v@gmail.com.

About the game

Meowline: Island Escape is a cat-themed island adventure and arrow-path puzzle game. Choose a safe move order, clear connected arrow paths from the board, and progress through island levels. The App includes campaign levels, daily activities, check-ins, a sticker collection, hints and guides, and optional ads.

A level is difficult or seems stuck

Look for an arrow path that can reach the edge of the board without crossing another remaining path. Clearing available arrows may open routes for the next moves. Hints and guides are available in the game where shown.

My progress is missing

Progress is stored locally on your device. The current App does not require an account and does not provide cloud-save recovery. Do not uninstall the App or clear its data while troubleshooting. In your email, tell us whether the issue happened after an update, reinstall, device change, or clearing app data.

An ad or reward is unavailable

Ads depend on your optional-services choice, internet connection, region, ad-provider inventory, and release configuration. Check your connection, wait a moment, and restart the App. A reward is granted only after an eligible ad completes successfully. You can still play the core puzzle game without optional services.

The App freezes or closes

Restart the App and device, check that you are using the latest available version, and send the steps that reproduce the problem. A screenshot or screen recording can help if it contains no personal or sensitive information.

Changing privacy choices

Use the App’s Settings to change or withdraw optional-services consent. On iOS, App Tracking Transparency is managed separately in iOS Settings. If an optional iOS web-service integration was already started, close and reopen the game after withdrawing consent to finish stopping it.

What to include in a support email

Please include your device model and operating-system version, App version/build if available, the level or daily activity involved, what you expected and what happened, and the steps to reproduce the issue. Please do not send passwords, payment-card details, government ID numbers, or other sensitive information. We cannot promise recovery of locally stored progress.

Privacy requests

For a privacy question or request, write “Privacy Request” in the subject and explain what you need. See the Privacy Policy for more information.
