Privacy Policy — Meowline: Arrow Escape

Effective date: October 24, 2026

Meowline: Arrow Escape (the “App”) is a colorful arrow-and-line puzzle game. For privacy questions or requests, contact leo0912v@gmail.com.

1. Information stored on your device

The App does not require an account, sign-in, phone number, or registration. Game progress and preferences are stored locally on your device, including campaign progress, energy, daily-challenge progress, collection and sticker state, tool inventories, sound, music, vibration, and other gameplay settings.

The current App does not provide account-based cloud save, account recovery, or an in-app purchase flow. If you delete the App or its local data, or change devices, locally stored progress may not be recoverable.

2. Advertising, attribution, and analytics

The App contains optional integrations that may be enabled in a particular release build through configuration:

  • AppLovin MAX may provide rewarded video advertising. If enabled, AppLovin and its mediated advertising partners may process device/app information, advertising identifiers, IP address, ad requests, impressions, interactions, reward events, and related technical information according to their policies.
  • AppsFlyer may provide install attribution, campaign measurement, session information, and ad-revenue measurement when configured.
  • GameAnalytics may provide gameplay analytics. The App can send progression start/complete/fail events, rewarded-ad events, and design or revenue events when this service is enabled.

The actual services, ad partners, data collected, and purposes depend on the release build, platform, region, SDK configuration, and vendor settings. The App does not claim that every service listed above is active in every build.

For more information, see the providers’ notices: AppLovin Privacy Policy, AppsFlyer Services Privacy Policy, and GameAnalytics Privacy Notice.

3. Consent and tracking permission

Before starting optional advertising, attribution, or analytics services, the App shows a first-launch notice describing those services and links to this policy and the Terms of Use. If you accept, configured AppLovin MAX, AppsFlyer, and GameAnalytics integrations may run as described above. If you continue without optional data, the puzzle game remains playable, while those optional integrations and their ads are disabled.

You can change this choice in the App’s Settings. Withdrawing consent stops AppsFlyer and GameAnalytics submissions and disables further ad requests in the App. Information already sent to a provider may remain subject to that provider’s retention and deletion practices. The integrations that are active depend on the release build and configuration.

On supported iOS versions, the App may separately request Apple’s App Tracking Transparency authorization before a configured attribution or advertising flow. This request is controlled by iOS and can be changed in iOS Settings. App Tracking Transparency is separate from the App’s privacy choice; declining ATT does not prevent local gameplay.

4. How information is used

Where applicable, information is used to:

  • save and restore gameplay progress and preferences on your device;
  • provide rewarded advertising and grant an in-game reward after a completed eligible ad;
  • measure advertising, installs, campaigns, and ad revenue;
  • understand puzzle progression and improve game stability or design; and
  • respond to support or privacy messages sent to the support email.

5. Sharing

The current App does not operate an account database or sell information through an account service. If optional SDKs are enabled, information may be shared with the relevant SDK provider, its service providers, and advertising partners to provide advertising, attribution, analytics, or measurement. Information included in an email is handled by the email services involved in sending and receiving it.

6. Deletion and privacy requests

Because the current App stores gameplay data locally and does not have accounts, you can generally remove local data by using device controls or deleting the App. Removing local data may also remove your progress.

Data processed by third-party providers is subject to their own retention and request procedures. For a privacy question or request, email leo0912v@gmail.com with “Privacy Request” in the subject. Please do not send passwords, payment-card details, government ID numbers, or other sensitive information by email.

7. Children

The App is a general-audience casual puzzle game. It contains no violence, sexual content, gambling, social features, user-generated content, or unrestricted web browser.

8. Security and international processing

We use reasonable safeguards for information under our control. No app, device, or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure. Third-party providers may process information in countries other than your own under their own policies and safeguards.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the App or its data practices change. We will update the effective date and publish the current version at the public Privacy Policy URL associated with the App.
