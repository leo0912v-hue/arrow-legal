About the game

Arrow Puzzle: Save the Animals is a casual arrow-path puzzle game. Tap an arrow with a clear route, remove its connected line, and clear the board by choosing the right move order. The App includes campaign levels, daily challenges, animal collections, stickers, tools, and optional rewarded ads.

A level is difficult or seems stuck

Look for an arrow whose path can reach the edge of the board without crossing another remaining line. Clear available arrows first; new routes can become available after earlier arrows are removed. Hints and other tools are available where shown in the App.

My progress is missing

Progress is stored locally on the device. The current App does not require an account and does not provide cloud-save recovery. Do not delete the App while troubleshooting. Tell us whether the issue happened after an update, reinstall, device change, or clearing app data.

A rewarded ad will not load

Rewarded ads depend on network access, regional availability, ad-provider inventory, and the release configuration. Check your connection, wait a moment, and restart the App. A reward is granted only after an eligible ad completes successfully.

The App freezes or closes

Restart the App and device, check that you are using the latest available version, and send the exact steps that reproduce the problem. A screenshot or screen recording can help if it contains no personal or sensitive information.

What to include in a support email

Please include:

  • device model and operating-system version;
  • App version/build, if available;
  • the level or daily challenge involved;
  • what you expected and what happened; and
  • the steps to reproduce the problem.

Please do not send passwords, payment-card details, government ID numbers, or other sensitive information. We cannot promise recovery of locally stored progress.
