Privacy Policy — Arrow Puzzle: Save the Animals

Effective date: October 16, 2026

Arrow Puzzle: Save the Animals (the “App”) is a casual arrow-path puzzle game. For privacy questions or requests, contact leo0912v@gmail.com.

1. Information stored on your device

The App does not require an account, sign-in, phone number, or registration. Game progress and preferences are stored locally on your device, including campaign progress, energy, daily-challenge progress, animal collections, stickers, tool inventories, sound, music, vibration, guidelines, and other gameplay settings.

The current App does not provide account-based cloud save, account recovery, or an in-app purchase flow. If you delete the App or its local data, or change devices, locally stored progress may not be recoverable.

2. Advertising, attribution, and analytics

A particular release may enable optional third-party services. The services used, information processed, and purposes depend on the release build, platform, region, and configuration:

  • AppLovin MAX may provide rewarded video advertising and may process device or app information, advertising identifiers, IP address, ad requests, impressions, interactions, reward events, and related technical information.
  • AppsFlyer may provide install attribution, campaign measurement, session information, and ad-revenue measurement when configured.
  • GameAnalytics may receive gameplay progression, rewarded-ad, design, and revenue events when enabled.

For more information, see the providers’ notices: AppLovin Privacy Policy, AppsFlyer Services Privacy Policy, and GameAnalytics Privacy Notice.

3. Tracking permission and choices

On supported iOS versions, the App may request Apple’s App Tracking Transparency authorization before a configured attribution flow starts. This request is controlled by iOS and can be changed later in Settings.

If tracking is not authorized, advertising or measurement may still use other permitted signals, and rewarded ads may still be available.

4. How information is used

Where applicable, information is used to:

  • save and restore gameplay progress and preferences on your device;
  • provide rewarded advertising and grant an in-game reward after an eligible ad completes;
  • measure advertising, installs, campaigns, and ad revenue;
  • understand puzzle progression and improve stability or game design; and
  • respond to support or privacy messages sent to the support email.

5. Sharing

The App does not operate an account database or sell information through an account service. If optional SDKs are enabled, information may be shared with the relevant SDK provider, its service providers, and advertising partners to provide advertising, attribution, analytics, or measurement. Information included in an email is handled by the email services involved in sending and receiving it.

6. Deletion and privacy requests

Because gameplay data is stored locally and the App has no accounts, you can generally remove local data through device controls or by deleting the App. This may also remove your progress.

Data processed by third-party providers is subject to their own retention and request procedures. For a privacy question or request, email leo0912v@gmail.com with “Privacy Request” in the subject. Please do not send passwords, payment-card details, government ID numbers, or other sensitive information by email.

7. Children

The App is a general-audience casual puzzle game. It contains no social feed, user-generated content, unrestricted web browser, gambling, or in-app chat. The final age rating and child-directed status will follow the applicable store and legal requirements for the release.

8. Security and international processing

We use reasonable safeguards for information under our control. No app, device, or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure. Third-party providers may process information in countries other than your own under their own policies and safeguards.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the App or its data practices change. We will update the effective date and publish the current version at the public Privacy Policy URL associated with the App.
